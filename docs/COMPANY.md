# Ridgeline Supply Co. (fictional)

Every project in this portfolio runs inside one **fictional** company, so the agents solve connected, realistic business problems instead of isolated demos. Ridgeline, its customers, suppliers, people, orders and numbers are all invented. All data is synthetic and generated by scripts in each project.

## The business
Ridgeline Supply Co. is a mid-size distributor of industrial safety and outdoor equipment, with about $400M in annual revenue and 1,100 employees.
- **Sells** B2B to big-box retailers over EDI (X12 850/855/856/810), to contractors through a sales team, and direct through a web store.
- **Ships** from three distribution centers (Reno NV, Columbus OH, Dallas TX) with parcel carriers and LTL freight.
- **Buys** from about 300 suppliers, with purchase orders, receipts and supplier invoices.
- **Bills and collects:** about 40,000 invoices a month, with lockbox remittances, short payments and retailer deductions.

## Systems landscape (all mocked or sandboxed in this repo)
| System | Role | How it's represented here |
|--------|------|---------------------------|
| ERP (SAP S/4HANA) | Sales orders, deliveries, billing, AP/AR, GL | SAP Business Accelerator Hub sandbox APIs + local mock ERP |
| CRM (Salesforce) | Accounts, cases, opportunities | Salesforce Developer Edition org (free) |
| TMS / carriers | Shipments, tracking, proof of delivery, freight invoices | Mock carrier and TMS APIs (FastAPI) |
| WMS | Picks, packs, cartons, receipts | Mock WMS events |
| EDI gateway | Retailer and supplier documents | Local X12 pipeline (Project 02) |
| Bank / lockbox | Remittance files | Synthetic BAI2-style remittance files |
| Event broker | Business events between systems | Solace PubSub+ Standard or Kafka (Docker) |
| Integration platform | APIs and flows | MCP gateway (Project 01), SAP Integration Suite (Project 07) |
