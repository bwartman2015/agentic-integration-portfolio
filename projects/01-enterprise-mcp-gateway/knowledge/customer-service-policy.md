# Customer service policy (synthetic)

## Returns
Unopened items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened safety equipment cannot be returned unless defective.

## Shipping SLAs
Ground orders placed before 2 pm local DC time ship the same business day. Standard ground delivery is 3-5 business days. Expedited is 2 business days.

## Billing disputes
If a customer was invoiced for quantity that has not been delivered, open a case with reason "billed not delivered". AR will hold dunning on that invoice for 15 days.
