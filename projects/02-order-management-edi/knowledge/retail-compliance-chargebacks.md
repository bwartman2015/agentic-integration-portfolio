# Retail compliance and chargebacks (synthetic)

- 855 acknowledgement due within 24 hours of the 850. Late = $250 chargeback per PO.
- 856 ASN must be sent before the truck arrives and match cartons exactly. Inaccurate ASN = 2% of invoice value.
- Price on 810 invoice must match the PO price. Mismatches create deductions in AR (see Project 04).
