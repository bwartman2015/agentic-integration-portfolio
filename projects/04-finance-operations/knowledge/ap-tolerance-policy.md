# AP matching tolerance policy (synthetic)

- Price variance: release if within 2% or $50 per invoice, whichever is lower. Above: hold and request a credit memo from the supplier.
- Quantity: never pay for quantity not received. Invoice qty > receipt qty -> hold until receipt or credit memo.
- Freight on supplier invoices: allowed only if the PO terms are FOB origin.
- Possible duplicates (same vendor, same amount, invoice date within 10 days): hold and confirm with the vendor.
